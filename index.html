Clean & Transparent

Ingredients & Allergen Information

A simple, transparent look at what goes into making Burger King french fries.

Key Ingredients

  • 1
    Whole Potatoes: High-grade Russet or Shepody potatoes selected for optimal starch content and moisture balance.
  • 2
    Crispy Starch Batter: A specialized coating made from potato starch, rice flour, and tapioca dextrin to enhance outer crunch.
  • 3
    Vegetable Oil Blend: Fried in zero-trans-fat canola, palm, and soybean oil mixture enriched with citric acid to preserve oil freshness.
  • 4
    Sea Salt: Lightly dusted with refined sea salt immediately upon removal from the fryer basket.

Allergen & Dietary Notice

Burger King fries do not contain direct dairy, eggs, meat products, or gluten in their base potato recipe. However, cross-contact considerations apply:

⚠️ Shared Fryer Notice

Burger King restaurant kitchens utilize shared deep fryers to prepare fries alongside gluten-containing products such as chicken tenders, onion rings, and fish fillets. Individuals with severe celiac disease or wheat/dairy allergies should exercise caution.
